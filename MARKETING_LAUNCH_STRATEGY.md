# DoorSurvey Pro - Marketing Launch Strategy
**Last Updated:** February 7, 2026
**Target Launch:** Q1 2026
**Goal:** 200 paying customers within 6 months

---

## Executive Summary

DoorSurvey Pro is positioned as a comprehensive SaaS platform purpose-built for commercial door professionals. This strategy targets the US commercial door industry where most firms still rely on paper-based surveys, spreadsheet estimations, and manual compliance checking. The platform's triple-scan LiDAR system, AI-powered compliance validation, and end-to-end project management create a defensible competitive advantage.

---

## 1. Market Positioning

### Value Proposition

**Primary Message:** "The complete platform for commercial door survey, estimation, and project management."

**Core Benefits (Target Claims - validate with customer data):**
- **Significantly faster surveys** with AR measurements and triple-scan LiDAR
- **Fewer specification errors** through AI-powered hardware identification and compliance validation
- **Professional quotes in minutes** instead of hours
- **Automatic code compliance** across NFPA 80, IBC, and ADA

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Weakness | DoorSurvey Pro Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheet workflows | No automation, error-prone | End-to-end digital workflow |
| Generic project management | No door industry expertise | Purpose-built with hardware catalog |
| Paper-based surveys | Slow, no real-time data | Mobile LiDAR + AR measurement |
| Basic estimating tools | No compliance checking | 95-rule automated compliance engine |

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Segment (70% of budget)

**Commercial Door Contractors (100-500 employees)**
- Pain points: Manual data entry, quote turnaround time, field-to-office disconnect
- Decision makers: Operations Manager, Project Manager
- Annual contract value: $5,000-$15,000
- Estimated US market: 3,000+ firms

**Locksmith & Security Companies (20-100 employees)**
- Pain points: Keying system complexity, hardware specification errors
- Decision makers: Owner, Lead Technician
- Annual contract value: $2,000-$8,000
- Estimated US market: 8,000+ firms

### Secondary Segment (20% of budget)

**Architectural Hardware Consultants (1-10 employees)**
- Pain points: Multi-project management, hardware research time
- Decision makers: Principal, Senior Consultant
- Annual contract value: $1,500-$5,000

### Tertiary Segment (10% of budget)

**General Contractors (200+ employees)**
- Pain points: Subcontractor coordination, door schedule management
- Decision makers: Division Manager, Superintendent
- Annual contract value: $10,000-$50,000

## 4. Customer Acquisition Channels

### Channel 1: Industry Trade Shows & Events (30% of budget)

**Target Events:**
- DHI (Door & Hardware Institute) Annual Conference
- ASIS International Security Conference
- AHC (Architectural Hardware Consultant) Regional Meetings
- CSI (Construction Specifications Institute) Events

**Strategy:**
- Live demo stations showing LiDAR scanning and AI compliance
- "Survey Challenge" comparing manual vs. DoorSurvey Pro speed
- QR code to free trial with show-specific landing page

### Channel 2: Digital Marketing (35% of budget)

**Search Engine Marketing:**
- Target keywords: "commercial door survey software", "door hardware estimating", "door compliance checking tool"
- Long-tail: "NFPA 80 door inspection checklist", "commercial door schedule template"
- Estimated CPC: $3-8 for industry keywords

**Content Marketing:**
- Weekly blog posts on compliance topics (SEO-driven)
- Monthly case studies from beta customers
- "Door Survey Field Guide" downloadable PDF (lead magnet)
- Video tutorials: LiDAR scanning, compliance checking, project setup

**Social Media:**
- LinkedIn: Primary B2B channel, industry group engagement
- YouTube: Product demos, feature tutorials, industry education

### Channel 3: Industry Partnerships (20% of budget)

**Hardware Manufacturers:**
- Co-marketing with major door hardware brands
- Manufacturer catalog integration as partnership value
- Joint webinars on compliance and specification

**Distributor Network:**
- Reseller program for door hardware distributors
- Volume licensing for distributor customer bases
- API integration with distributor ordering systems

**Industry Associations:**
- DHI endorsed technology partner status
- AHC recommended tools listing
- Continuing education content partnerships

### Channel 4: Referral & Word of Mouth (15% of budget)

**Referral Program:**
- Existing customer refers new customer: 1 month free for both
- Tiered rewards: Bronze (1 referral), Silver (3), Gold (5+)
- Automated tracking via HubSpot CRM integration

**Beta Program:**
- Private beta with single-use invitation tokens
- Beta tester feedback loop with in-app bug reporting
- Early adopter pricing locked for 24 months

## 6. Sales Process

### Funnel Stages

1. **Awareness:** Blog, trade shows, ads, referrals
2. **Interest:** Free trial signup (14 days, no credit card)
3. **Evaluation:** Guided demo with sales team, sandbox environment
4. **Decision:** Custom proposal based on team size and needs
5. **Onboarding:** Branding wizard, team invites, first project setup

### Conversion Metrics (Targets)
| Stage | Target Rate |
|---|---|
| Visit to Trial | 5% |
| Trial to Demo | 30% |
| Demo to Paid | 40% |
| Monthly Churn | <3% |

### Sales Enablement
- Interactive demo environment (pre-seeded with sample data)
- ROI calculator showing time/cost savings
- Competitive comparison sheets
- Customer testimonial library
- Proposal templates with configurable pricing

## 10. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low trial conversion | A/B test onboarding flow, add guided tours |
| High CPA | Shift budget to organic content, referrals |
| Churn in first 90 days | Dedicated onboarding specialist, check-in cadence |
| Competitor response | Accelerate feature development, deepen integrations |
| Trade show ROI unclear | Track leads with unique landing pages per event |
