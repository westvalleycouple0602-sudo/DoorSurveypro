# DoorSurvey Pro – Door Opening Survey Workflow

## 1. Project Initialization

**Goal:** Establish project context and applicable building codes before any doors are surveyed.

**Fields**
- Project name  
- Location  
- Customer (optional)  
- Building type (Hospital, Office, School, etc.)  
- Quote type (None, Budget, Proposal, Change Order)  
- Applicable standards (ADA, IBC, NFPA, ANSI/SDI, Local)

---

## 2. Opening Mapping

**Goal:** Link each opening to its location on the floor plan and assign a stable ID.

**Steps**
1. Select floor plan.
2. Drop a marker on the door location.
3. Assign or confirm door tag (e.g., `101A`).
4. Enter floor and space (e.g., Level 1 – Corridor 1).
5. Select opening type and handing.

---

## 3. AR / LiDAR Measurements

**Goal:** Capture accurate dimensions of the opening.

**Captured**
- Rough opening width & height  
- Clear width  
- Head height  
- Jamb depth  
- Door thickness  
- Sill height (if applicable)

The app guides the user with AR prompts (start at hinge jamb, sweep across head, etc.) and validates measurements against code tolerances.

---

## 4. Door Panel Survey

**Goal:** Describe the installed or proposed door leaf.

**Captured**
- Door type (flush, full glass, half glass, narrow lite)  
- Core type (solid, hollow, mineral, STC)  
- Fire rating (minutes)  
- Vision lite presence and description  
- Manufacturer and model (barcode or manual selection)

---

## 5. Frame Survey

**Goal:** Capture frame material, profile, anchorage, and condition.

**Captured**
- Frame material (hollow metal, aluminum, wood)  
- Profile size  
- Anchor type (existing, new, steel clip)  
- Condition score (1–5)  
- Notes and special conditions  

---

## 6. Hardware Set

**Goal:** Identify all hardware installed or required at the opening.

**Captured**
- Hinges (size, type, quantity, manufacturer)  
- Lock/exit device details and function code  
- Closer type and mounting  
- Accessories (kick plates, stops, coordinators, etc.)  
- Electrified hardware (mag locks, power supplies, readers, operators)

The app cross-references the hardware catalog and suggests code-compliant substitutions.

---

## 7. Code Compliance

**Goal:** Automatically analyze the opening against codes and standards.

**Standards Checked**
- ADA: clear width, approaches, hardware heights, opening force  
- IBC: fire rating, glass type, egress requirements  
- NFPA 80: fire door assembly details  
- ANSI/SDI: frame and door standards  

**Output**
- Pass/Fail status  
- List of issues with code references  
- Recommendations for corrective actions  

---

## 8. Photos & Evidence

**Goal:** Capture visual evidence for engineering review and record keeping.

**Captured**
- Full elevation photo  
- Frame and hinge detail  
- Lock/exit device close-up  
- Threshold/undercut photo  
- Fire label and listing marks  

---

## 9. Engineering Review

**Goal:** Provide a structured handoff to engineering.

**Captured**
- AI-generated summary  
- Reviewer status (Pending, Approved, Changes Required)  
- Identified conflicts (hardware, rating, egress, structure)  
- Reviewer notes  

---

## 10. Quote Summary

**Goal:** Turn the survey into a priceable opening.

**Captured**
- Material cost  
- Labor cost  
- Shipping cost  
- Margin %  
- Total price  
- Option label (Base / Option A / Option B)  

---

## 11. Final Report & Export

**Goal:** Produce deliverables for the client and internal teams.

**Outputs**
- Survey report (PDF)  
- Compliance summary (PDF / JSON)  
- Quote breakdown (PDF / CSV)  
- Data export (JSON) to integrate with estimating, ERP, and CRM systems
